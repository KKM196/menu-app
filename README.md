**Community Running Event Registration and Management System**

A local athletics club hosts an annual community running event comprising four race distances, namely the 5 km Fun Run, 10 km Race, 21.1 km Half Marathon, and 42.2 km Marathon. The current registration process is paper-based and has resulted in lost registration forms, duplicate registrations, incorrect fee calculations, difficulty determining participant numbers per race, slow generation of reports, poor management of participant information, and an inability to accurately track event income.
The organising committee has appointed me as a junior software developer to design and develop a computerised registration and management system for the event.
